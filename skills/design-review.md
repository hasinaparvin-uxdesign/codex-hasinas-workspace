# Review the design

Locate the actual artifact and version, brief, context, and owner principles before asking for files already available. If the artifact is absent, report what cannot be inspected. Assess hierarchy, task clarity, interaction, accessibility, responsive behavior, appropriate themes, and relevant edge states.

For each dimension use Pass, Needs work, Fail, or Not verified according to observed evidence. Record the element, finding, user impact, specific fix, and source/version in the selected critique notes. Compute contrast only from known colors; distinguish the calculation from rendered evidence. Verify standard-specific thresholds instead of treating a font-size or target-size preference as a universal requirement.

Prioritize meaningful user impact. Keep recommendations separate from owner decisions and actual implementation. A screenshot cannot prove keyboard, screen-reader, runtime, or real-user outcomes. Preserve historical reviews and identify a new review's scope rather than overwriting their evidence.
