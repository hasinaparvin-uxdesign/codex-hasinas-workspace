# Stress-test perspectives

Read the actual artifact, task, context, brief, and owner principles. Use validated research personas if available. Otherwise label the perspectives as hypothetical evaluation lenses, not customers observed in research.

Consider a first-time or confused user, an implementer checking feasibility and states, and a product owner checking the agreed outcome. Adapt these lenses to the real project; do not replace a broad audience with invented demographic targeting.

For each lens, identify specific friction, evidence, the possible consequence, and a proposed fix. Distinguish visible evidence, inference, and what needs testing. Consolidate overlaps and conflicts in the project's critique notes, retaining the owner's final decision and actual implementation status. Never invent results, metrics, interviews, or agreement among real users. A recreated HTML critique is not an update to the source design.
