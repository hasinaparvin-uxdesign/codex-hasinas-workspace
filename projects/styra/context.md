# Styra — project context

**Status: owner-confirmed direction with research gaps.** Styra is the AI Shopping Assistant, not a second project. The [project index](README.md#owner-confirmed-direction) is the source for the owner's confirmed direction.

Customers should save time finding approximately three to four relevant products within their budget across brands, non-branded shops, marketplaces, and second-hand sellers. Intended coverage includes clothing, shoes, bags, and accessories; cosmetics remain a possibility. The audience is broad across genders and professions.

Apply the [project agreement](codex-contract.md): include delivery cost, seller reliability, return conditions, uncertainty disclosure, and explicit approval before any order or payment.

No validated primary segment, device floor, launch market, language, research sample, or integration is established by this context. The older [V1 brief](brief-v1-client.md) remains a draft. Its clothing and euro examples, and the personas in historical critiques, are not research or final launch constraints.

Brief expansion is deferred. Voice scope, the purchase handoff, initial categories, and data feasibility remain owner decisions for a later design task. Reference-repository drafts do not supersede the confirmed direction here.
