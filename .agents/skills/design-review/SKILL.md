# Review the design

Use this repository workflow for the selected project or career task. Read the applicable [repository instructions](../../../AGENTS.md), then follow the canonical [design-review procedure](../../../skills/design-review.md).

Read the relevant agreements and context before substantive work. The procedure does not grant additional authority to change settled product decisions or perform external actions. Preserve draft, evidence, implementation, and approval status. Do not expand Styra's deferred brief unless the owner explicitly resumes that work.
